Problem Statement

  • Severe Medical Risks: Prescription reading errors are a leading cause of patient harm, wrong dosages, and severe drug-drug interactions globally.
  • Highly Challenging Environments: Pharmacists work under extreme time constraints, manual workflows, and must decipher illegible handwritten prescriptions.
  • High Costs & Alert Fatigue: Existing Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS) are prohibitively expensive, require active internet connections, and suffer from notification overload.

Proposed Solution

  • Automated Offline System: A lightweight, thick-client desktop application running fully offline, ensuring maximum data privacy and instantaneous verification speed.
  • Hybrid Safety Verification: Employs state-of-the-art OCR (PaddleOCR) for printed & handwritten script reading, integrated with a trained Machine Learning similarity model and DrugBank knowledge rules.
  • Optimized Pharmacist Workflow: Automatically generates patient-friendly bilingual instructions (English & Urdu) and integrates automated inventory alerts & analytics.

Key System Features

OCR Extraction

Recognizes handwritten/printed scripts with up to 97% accuracy using PaddleOCR.

AI Interaction Detection

Trained ML similarity model checks drug-drug interactions with high confidence.

Bilingual Instructions

Instantly generates clear patient dosage advice in both English and Urdu (EN/UR).

Interactive Analytics

Tracks prescription volume, error rates, and verification latency in real-time.

Inventory Alerts

Automatically triggers low-stock & medicine expiry alerts with zero lag.

Security & Audit Logs

Fully offline encryption (AES-256) with complete audit trail for compliance.
